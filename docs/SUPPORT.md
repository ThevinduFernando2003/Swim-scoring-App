# Support plan

Agree this **in writing** before handover. It is not code; it is who does what when something breaks.

Fill in the blanks with the people on both sides.

## After handover

| Kind of work | Who | How long |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Hosting / DNS / domain | Receiving organisation (Vercel owner) | Ongoing |
| Database, logins, backups | Receiving organisation (Supabase owner) | Ongoing |
| AI bill for PDF reading | Receiving organisation | Ongoing |
| Bug that blocks scoring on meet day | _Name / org_ | _e.g. 30 days after handover, best-effort_ |
| Non-urgent bugs | _Name / org_ | _e.g. next available weekend_ |
| New features (extra reports, new sports) | _Out of scope unless a new agreement_ | — |

## What “support” is not

- Building a new meet type that needs a different scoring system, unless agreed separately.
- Training every official in person unless scheduled.
- Paying OpenAI / Vercel / Supabase invoices after accounts are transferred.

- **Technical contact:** _email_
